# Business Insights

## Executive Metrics

The analytical model contains 99,441 orders and 96,096 unique customers.

Product revenue is R$13.59M, while freight value is R$2.25M, producing a gross order value of approximately R$15.84M.

There are 96,478 delivered orders.

## Delivery Performance

7,827 orders are flagged as late deliveries.

The late-delivery rate is approximately 8.11% of delivered orders.

## Customer Value

The customer-value model identifies eight segments:

- Hibernating
- Needs Attention
- At Risk - Valuable
- Recent Customers
- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- At Risk - High Value
- High Potential

The At Risk - High Value segment contains 1,653 customers with an average monetary value of approximately R$698.78.

This segment can be used as a focused customer-retention monitoring population.

## Revenue Risk Signals

The order-risk model identifies:

- 1,076 payment mismatch flags
- 1,231 cancelled/unavailable paid-order signals
- 7,827 late-delivery flags
- 15,610 high-freight-ratio flags
- 2,961 multiple-payment flags
- 14,449 negative-review signals

A total of 8,267 orders contain multiple risk signals.

These are analytical screening indicators and should not be interpreted as confirmed fraud or financial loss.

## Category Performance

The highest-revenue categories include:

- Health & Beauty
- Watches & Gifts
- Bed Bath Table
- Sports Leisure
- Computers Accessories

Freight burden varies across categories, making freight-to-revenue ratio useful alongside revenue volume.

## Seller Performance

Seller performance combines revenue, order volume, delivery performance and customer feedback.

This allows high-volume sellers to be compared with their delivery and review indicators.

## Business Use

The resulting dashboard can support:

- Executive revenue monitoring
- Customer retention analysis
- Operational delivery monitoring
- Seller performance reviews
- Category-level performance analysis
- Risk investigation prioritization
